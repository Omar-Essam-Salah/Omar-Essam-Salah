I'm **Omar Essam Salah** (عُمَر) — an **IT Systems Administrator** by day, and by night I design and ship
complete systems from scratch across **mobile & web apps**, **embedded hardware**, and **real-time,
on-device AI**. Different domains, one through-line: systems that run **offline, private, and

for the sake of Allah.

I don't stop at a demo. I choose and wire the real hardware, own the architecture, and take projects all
the way to something that actually runs.

## Featured work

### [Nur — Quran & Azkar](https://github.com/Omar-Essam-Salah/Nur-Quran-Azkar)
A free, ad-free, fully **offline** Quran & Azkar app — heading to Google Play. The complete Uthmani Quran
with a byte-level integrity check, a paper-style Mushaf and a verse-by-verse reader, word-by-word tap
(meaning plus audio for each word), many reciters with live highlighting, on-device prayer times and
adhan, Qibla, azkar with sources, a duʿāʾ library, hadith from nine authentic Sunni collections, a
memorization companion, and a multi-language interface — while the Quran text always stays in Arabic.
*React · TypeScript · Vite · Tailwind · Capacitor (Android)*

### [COOPER — Offline AI Presence](https://github.com/Omar-Essam-Salah/cooper-ai)
An ambient, **fully offline** AI companion: a local LLM with real-time speech, distributed across ESP32
edge nodes (mic array, display, custom power rails) and a GPU "brain" over a local message bus — no
cloud, private by design. My most ambitious project; published here as a concept and engineering
showcase.
*Local LLM (Ollama / Qwen) · faster-whisper STT · Piper TTS · Silero VAD · ESP32-S3 mic-array · UDP mesh*

### [Handball AI — Real-Time Tactical Analysis](https://github.com/Omar-Essam-Salah/handball-ai-system)
A real-time system that reads handball broadcasts from a 4K camera: it tracks every player and the ball,
detects events (pass / shot / goal / save / fast break), and generates per-goal tactical replays and
match analytics — 100% offline on CUDA. Portfolio showcase (proprietary).
*Python · YOLOv8 · OpenCV · PyTorch · OSNet Re-ID · Kalman · FastAPI + WebSocket*

### [esp32-retro-console](https://github.com/Omar-Essam-Salah/esp32-retro-console)
A handheld retro game console built from scratch — custom **ESP32-S3** hardware plus firmware that boots
to an SD-card ROM browser and runs multiple systems (NES / Game Boy / Master System / Mega Drive), with
Wi-Fi ROM upload straight to the SD card.
*C++ · ESP32-S3 · PlatformIO · I2S audio · ST7789 SPI · FreeRTOS*

### Enterprise IT & local-AI infrastructure
Day to day I administer and automate IT infrastructure, and I build **local-AI** stacks that keep data
in-house: offline RAG pipelines, self-hosted LLMs with Ollama, and secure, automated networks.
